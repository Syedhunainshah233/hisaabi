Aap ka lena dena, saaf aur mehfooz.

Hisaabi is a simple khata (ledger) app for Android. Record money you gave and got, see who owes what, and get daily and monthly reports.

What Hisaabi does

  • Create a khata for each customer, supplier, friend or family member
  • Record Diya (gave) and Liya (got) entries with date and note
  • See how much you will get and how much you will give, at a glance
  • Daily, weekly and monthly reports with charts
  • Share PDF statements and WhatsApp reminders
  • English and Roman Urdu, dark mode, PIN lock

Your data stays with you

Hisaabi has no server of its own. Your records are saved on your phone. If you choose to sign in with Google, backups go to a private app folder in your own Google Drive.
