Privacy Policy

Effective date: 21 November 2026

ENGLISH

Who we are

Hisaabi ("the app") is an Android money ledger (khata) app developed by Syed Hunain ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the app handles and how. Contact: syedhunainshah120@gmail.com.

Information stored on your device

The ledgers and entries you create (names, phone numbers, amounts, dates, notes, address and the app settings, including a hashed PIN) are stored only in the app's private storage on your phone. We do not run a server and we do not receive, read or sell this data.

Google Sign-In and Google Drive backup (optional)

If you choose to sign in with Google, the app uses your Google account only to back up and restore your Hisaabi data. The app requests:

  • your basic profile (name, email address, profile picture), to show which account is connected;
  • access to the hidden app data folder in your Google Drive (drive.appdata), to save and restore your Hisaabi backup.

The app cannot see, open or change any of your other Google Drive files. Backups are stored in your own Google account, not on our servers. You can sign out at any time, and you can delete the backup from Google Drive (Settings › Manage apps).

Hisaabi's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. We do not use Google user data for advertising, and we do not sell it or transfer it to anyone.

Contacts

If you tap "Contacts" while creating a ledger, Android lets you pick one contact. The app reads only that contact's name and phone number and saves them in the ledger. It does not read your whole contact list.

Sharing you start

When you share a PDF statement, a CSV export or a WhatsApp reminder, the app hands the file or message to the app you choose (for example WhatsApp or Gmail). What happens next is covered by that app's own privacy policy.

Advertising

Some versions of the app may show ads through Google AdMob. If ads are shown, AdMob may collect device information such as the advertising ID, IP address and app usage to show and measure ads, as described in Google's advertising policy. Where required, the app will ask for your consent first. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings. Your ledger data is never shared with advertisers.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Data retention and deletion

Your data stays on your phone until you delete it in the app or uninstall the app. Google Drive backups stay in your Google account until you delete them. Because we hold no copy, we cannot recover data you delete.

Security

Data is kept in the app's private storage, which other apps cannot read. You can turn on a PIN lock. Please also keep your phone and Google account secure.

Changes

We may update this policy. The new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

ROMAN URDU

Hum kaun hain

Hisaabi ek Android khata app hai jo Syed Hunain ne banayi hai. Yeh policy batati hai ke app kaunsi maloomat rakhti hai aur kaise. Rabta: syedhunainshah120@gmail.com.

Aap ke phone mein data

Aap ke banaye hue khatay aur entries (naam, number, raqam, tareekh, note, address aur settings) sirf aap ke phone mein app ki private jagah par save hote hain. Hamara koi server nahi. Hum yeh data na dekhte hain, na lete hain, na bechte hain.

Google login aur Drive backup (marzi se)

Agar aap Google se login karte hain to app aap ka account sirf backup aur data wapas laane ke liye use karti hai. App aap ka naam, email aur photo leti hai (taake pata chale kaunsa account juda hai), aur aap ki Google Drive ka sirf chhupa hua app folder use karti hai. App aap ki Drive ki baqi files nahi dekh sakti. Backup aap ke apne Google account mein rehta hai. Google API se mili maloomat Google API Services User Data Policy (Limited Use) ke mutabiq hi use hoti hai, ads ke liye nahi, aur kisi ko nahi di jaati.

Contacts

"Contacts" dabane par aap sirf ek contact chunte hain. App sirf us contact ka naam aur number leti hai, poori contact list nahi.

Share karna

PDF, CSV ya WhatsApp reminder bhejte waqt file us app ko jaati hai jo aap chunte hain. Us ke baad us app ki apni privacy policy lagu hoti hai.

Ads

App ke kuch versions mein Google AdMob ke ads aa sakte hain. Is soorat mein AdMob device ki maloomat (jaise advertising ID) le sakta hai. Zaroorat hui to app pehle aap se ijazat maangegi. Aap ka hisaab ka data kabhi advertisers ko nahi diya jaata.

Data mitana

Data tab tak phone mein rehta hai jab tak aap app mein mita na dein ya app uninstall na kar dein. Google Drive ka backup aap ke account mein rehta hai jab tak aap khud na mitayein. Hamare paas koi copy nahi hoti.
