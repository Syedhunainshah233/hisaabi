ENGLISH

Where your data is

Hisaabi has no server and no Hisaabi account. Your ledgers and entries are stored only on your phone. If you signed in with Google, a backup file is also kept in a hidden app folder of your own Google Drive. SyedTechdevelopers does not hold a copy of your data.

Delete data on your phone

  • One entry: open the ledger, tap the entry, tap the delete icon.
  • One ledger and all its entries: open the ledger, tap ⋮ (top right), then Delete.
  • Everything: uninstall Hisaabi, or go to phone Settings › Apps › Hisaabi › Storage › Clear data.

Delete your Google Drive backup

  1. Open drive.google.com on a computer and sign in with the same Google account.
  2. Click the gear icon › Settings › Manage apps.
  3. Find Hisaabi, click Options › Delete hidden app data.
  4. Optional: click Disconnect from Drive, and in Hisaabi go to Settings › Logout.

What is deleted and what is kept

The steps above permanently delete your ledgers, entries, notes and settings. Nothing is kept by SyedTechdevelopers, because we never receive this data. Ad data collected by Google AdMob is handled under Google's policies; you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's Google settings.
